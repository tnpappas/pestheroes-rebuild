Repair Services

Structural repair for damage caused by pests and moisture

Repair the Damage, Protect Your Investment

Termites, rodents, and moisture can cause significant damage to your home's structure. Pest Heroes provides repair services to fix the damage and prevent future problems. From insulation replacement to structural wood repair, we handle it all.

Insulation Replacement

Rodents often damage attic and crawlspace insulation by tunneling, nesting, and contaminating it with droppings and urine. We remove damaged insulation, sanitize the area, and install new insulation to restore your home's energy efficiency and air quality.

Structural Wood Repair

Termite damage and moisture rot can compromise structural beams, floor joists, and sill plates. Our technicians repair or replace damaged wood to restore your home's structural integrity.

Crawlspace Restoration

Moisture and pest damage in crawlspaces can lead to fungal growth, wood decay, and poor air quality throughout your home. We provide complete crawlspace restoration including debris removal, sanitizing, vapor barrier installation, and structural repair.
