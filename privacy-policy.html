Privacy Policy

How Pest Heroes collects, uses, and protects your information

Information We Collect

Pest Heroes collects information you provide directly to us when you request an inspection, contact us, or use our services. This may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about your pest problem.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, schedule and perform pest control services, send service reports, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

We may share your information with service partners who help us deliver our services (such as our inspection partner, Safe House Property Inspections). We only share information necessary to provide the requested services.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us at 757-367-8342 to exercise these rights.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Call us at 757-367-8342 or use our contact form.
