The Threat to Your Home

Termites are a serious threat to Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads homes, particularly during our humid coastal Virginia climate. Subterranean termites can cause thousands of dollars in structural damage before you even know they're there. If you're buying or selling a home, a WDI (Wood-Destroying Insect) inspection is likely required for your real estate transaction.

WDI Inspections for Real Estate

Our certified inspectors provide thorough WDI inspections required for real estate transactions, with written reports with photos. We inspect the full interior including all visible and accessible areas, the garage, crawlspace, exterior of the home, and any outbuildings required for the transaction. In the event an issue is found, Pest Heroes can offer a solution to remedy the problem and keep your transaction moving smoothly.

Termite Treatment

If termites are found, our licensed technicians provide fast, effective treatment using the latest termiticide technology. We eliminate colonies and protect your home's structural integrity with proven methods designed for the specific conditions of Hampton Roads.

Why Hampton Roads Homes Are at Risk

Our coastal Virginia climate, high water table, and humidity create ideal conditions for subterranean termites. Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and the surrounding areas are all considered high-risk zones for termite activity. Annual inspections are recommended to catch infestations early before structural damage occurs.
