Same-day reports to keep your real estate closing on track

Keep Your Closing On Track

Are you buying or selling a home in Virginia Beach, Norfolk, or Chesapeake? Our certified inspectors provide thorough WDI (Wood-Destroying Insect) inspections required for real estate transactions, with same-day reports available.

What Our Inspection Covers

This inspection covers a full interior inspection to all visible and accessible areas including the garage and crawlspace, the exterior of the home, and any outbuildings required for the transaction. We document our findings in a clear, professional report that satisfies lender and buyer requirements.

Partnership with Safe House

We're partnered with Safe House Property Inspections, which has performed over 23,000 home inspections throughout Hampton Roads. This partnership gives you access to comprehensive inspection services and expert knowledge of local construction and pest patterns.

Found Termites? We Can Help

In the event an issue is found, Pest Heroes can offer a solution to remedy the problem and keep your transaction moving smoothly. We provide fast, effective termite treatment using the latest termiticide technology.
