WDI Inspection FAQ

How long is a termite (WDI) report good for in Hampton Roads?

The NPMA-33 form says the report is invalid for a mortgage or settlement if not used within 90 days of the inspection. In our experience, local closings rarely accept a report older than 30 days because of Hampton Roads moisture conditions, and lenders and title companies vary, so confirm their window before you schedule.

What does Section IV on a termite report mean?

Section IV of the NPMA-33 lists areas that were obstructed and not visible to the inspector, using number codes from the key on the form. Codes you will see often include 7 stored items, 11 limited access, 14 cluttered condition, 1 fixed ceiling, 16 dense vegetation and 25 spray foam insulation.

Can the inspector come back after obstructed areas are cleared?

Yes. If obstructed areas are cleared later, the inspection company can come back for another look. A fee may apply.

Is a termite report required for VA loans in Virginia?

Yes. VA purchase loans in Virginia require a wood destroying insect report, which is one reason the report's timing matters for closings here.
