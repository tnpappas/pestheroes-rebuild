Our Story

Pest Heroes is your go-to pest control solution for homeowners and real estate professionals throughout Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads. Our state-licensed technicians have successfully protected thousands of homes from termites, mosquitoes, ants, rodents, and moisture-related pest problems common in our coastal Virginia climate.

Whether you're a Virginia Beach homeowner dealing with a sudden ant invasion, a Norfolk real estate agent needing a fast WDI inspection for closing, or a Chesapeake resident looking for year-round pest prevention, Pest Heroes delivers customized solutions backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
