Why Here Is Different

Hampton Roads pests aren't textbook pests. Here's what our coastline actually does.

Termite Swarm Season Starts Early Here

Subterranean termite swarms in Hampton Roads typically begin in early spring, sooner than in cooler inland climates, because our soil temperatures warm up faster along the coast. A warm, wet winter can push swarm activity even earlier, which is why we inspect year-round instead of only in peak season.

Crawlspace Humidity from a High Water Table

Much of Virginia Beach, Norfolk, and Chesapeake sits on a high water table, which means crawlspaces stay damp even in dry weather. That constant moisture is exactly what termites, carpenter ants, and mold need to establish themselves under your home.

Mosquito Pressure from Back Bay & Lynnhaven

The wetlands around Back Bay and the Lynnhaven River watershed are natural mosquito breeding grounds. Properties near these areas see heavier mosquito pressure through a longer season than inland Virginia, which is why seasonal treatment timing matters more here than in a generic program.
