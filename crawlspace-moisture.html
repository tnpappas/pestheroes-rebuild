The Crawlspace Problem in Hampton Roads

Moisture problems in crawlspaces are extremely common in Hampton Roads due to our coastal climate and high water table. Excess moisture attracts termites, causes fungal growth, compromises your home's structural integrity, and affects the air quality inside your home. A clean, dry crawlspace is essential for protecting your investment.

Our Crawlspace Solutions

Pest Heroes provides comprehensive crawlspace solutions including dehumidifier installation, sump pump systems, vapor barrier installation, and structural repair services. We address the root cause of moisture issues to protect your home long-term, not just the symptoms.

Why Moisture Matters

Crawlspace moisture doesn't stay in the crawlspace. Up to 50% of the air in your home comes from the crawlspace, meaning moisture, mold spores, and musty odors enter your living space. By controlling crawlspace moisture, you're protecting your family's health and your home's value.

Comprehensive Approach

We don't just install a dehumidifier and leave. We assess the full picture: drainage, ventilation, vapor barriers, insulation, structural condition, and pest activity. Then we design a customized plan that addresses the root cause of your moisture issues.
