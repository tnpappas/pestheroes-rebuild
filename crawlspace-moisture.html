The Crawlspace Problem in Hampton Roads

Moisture problems in crawlspaces are extremely common in Hampton Roads due to our coastal climate and high water table. Excess moisture attracts termites, causes fungal growth, compromises your home's structural integrity, and affects the air quality inside your home. A clean, dry crawlspace is essential for protecting your investment.

Why Moisture Matters

Crawlspace moisture doesn't stay in the crawlspace. Up to 50% of the air in your home comes from the crawlspace, meaning moisture, mold spores, and musty odors enter your living space. By controlling crawlspace moisture, you're protecting your family's health and your home's value.

Moisture and termites go together

Damp wood draws termites, and the damage can look a lot like rot. Pick your question:

What draws termites in? Damage or wood rot?

What draws termites in?

Six things around the house that invite termites. The numbers on the photo match the list.

  1. Mulch or soil piled against the foundation
  2. Firewood stacked against the house
  3. Wood touching the ground
  4. Water pooling at the foundation
  5. Damp crawlspace or wood scraps under the house
  6. Branches or shrubs touching the siding

Termite damage or wood rot?

It can be both. Termites like damp wood, and rot can set in after termites damage it.

Termite damage

  • Hollow tunnels that follow the grain
  • Dried mud packed in the tunnels
  • Often hidden behind a solid surface

Wood rot

  • Caused by fungi and moisture
  • Cracks into small cubes or turns soft
  • Not covered by a termite report
