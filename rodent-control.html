Rodent Control & Exclusion Programs

We don't just eliminate rodents, we keep them from coming back

The Hampton Roads Rodent Problem

Rats and mice are a serious problem for Norfolk, Virginia Beach, and Hampton Roads homeowners, particularly during fall and winter when they seek indoor shelter, and after heavy rains when outdoor habitats flood. Rats contaminate food, damage property, chew electrical wiring, and carry diseases.

Complete Exclusion Approach

Our rodent control programs don't just eliminate existing mouse and rat populations. We identify and seal entry points to prevent future infestations. This comprehensive exclusion approach is essential for Hampton Roads homes, where rodents seek shelter during winter months and after heavy rains.

Our Process

We inspect your property to identify rodent activity and entry points, eliminate the existing population, seal access points, and provide ongoing monitoring. We also address conditions that attract rodents, such as accessible food sources and nesting materials.

Signs You May Have Rodents

Hearing scratching sounds in your walls or attic? Finding droppings in your kitchen or pantry? Noticing chewed wires, food packaging, or structural materials? Seeing rodent activity at dusk or dawn? These are all signs it's time to call Pest Heroes.
