Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Hampton Roads homeowners ask us most

What areas does Pest Heroes serve?

We serve Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, Portsmouth, Suffolk, and Isle of Wight. If you're in Hampton Roads and have a pest problem, we can help.

Do you offer free inspections?

Yes, we offer free pest control inspections. Contact us at 757-367-8342 or fill out our contact form to schedule yours.

What is a WDI inspection and do I need one?

A WDI (Wood-Destroying Insect) inspection is required for most real estate transactions in Virginia. It covers a full interior and exterior inspection for termites and other wood-destroying insects. We offer same-day reports to keep your closing on track.

Is your treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use environmentally safe treatment options and integrated pest management (IPM) techniques that protect your family and pets. Our move-in treatment is specifically designed to be child and pet friendly.

What is your satisfaction guarantee?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied with our service, we'll re-treat your home at no additional cost. All warranties are transferable to new homeowners.

How often should I get pest control treatments?

We offer quarterly, bi-monthly, and monthly seasonal pest control programs. The right frequency depends on your property and pest pressure. We'll recommend the best plan for your home during your inspection.

How quickly can you respond?

We respond to all inspection requests within one business day. For urgent pest problems, call 757-367-8342 for same-day or next-day service.

Are you licensed?

Yes, all our technicians are state-licensed and have 20+ years of combined experience protecting Hampton Roads properties.

Do you offer move-in treatments?

Yes, we offer an exclusive move-in pest control treatment paired with your termite and moisture inspection. This creates a safe, odorless protective barrier that prevents common pests from entering your new home.

Still Have Questions?

We're happy to help. Call us and we'll answer any pest question you have.
