How can I keep pests, rodents, and wildlife from getting into my home?

Our exclusion services help homeowners in Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, and across Hampton Roads stop pests before they start. We professionally seal vents, repair or replace crawlspace doors, and close off hidden entry points that rodents, insects, and other intruders use to get inside. A properly sealed home not only prevents infestations but also improves air quality and energy efficiency.

What are the most common wood destroying insects in Hampton Roads, and how do I protect my home?

Homes in our coastal region are vulnerable to termites, carpenter ants, wood borers, powder post beetles, and carpenter bees. Our licensed Pest Heroes team inspects, identifies, and treats active infestations using proven methods that protect your home's structure. We also offer preventative treatments to ensure long term protection from costly wood damage.

Can mosquitoes really be controlled around my yard in Virginia Beach or Chesapeake?

Yes, while mosquitoes can't be completely eradicated, our mosquito control services greatly reduce their population. We identify and treat breeding zones, apply safe and effective barrier sprays, and customize a plan for your property. The result is fewer bites and more time to enjoy your yard during the warmer months in Hampton Roads.

What's the best way to get rid of rats or mice in my home?

The most effective solution is a combination of baiting and exclusion repairs. Our rodent control programs are customized for each home in Virginia Beach, Norfolk, and Chesapeake. We seal vents, gaps, and other travel paths, then strategically place bait stations to stop rodents at the source. This dual approach eliminates current infestations and prevents new ones from returning.

How do I safely remove bees or wasps from my property?

If you have bees, wasps, yellow jackets, or hornets nesting near your home, it's best to call a professional. Our bee and wasp removal services protect your family by safely removing active hives and preventing new colonies from forming. We serve all parts of Hampton Roads and use environmentally responsible methods that balance safety with care for beneficial pollinators.
