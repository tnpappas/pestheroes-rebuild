Seasonal Pest Control FAQ

How can I tell carpenter bee damage from carpenter ant damage?

Carpenter bees leave a perfectly round hole about 1/2 inch wide with sawdust piled below it, usually in bare, weathered wood like decks, fascia and railings. Carpenter ants show up as large black ants indoors, often at night, with sawdust mixed with insect parts and smooth, clean tunnels in damp wood.

Do carpenter bees and carpenter ants eat wood?

No. Neither one eats wood. Both tunnel into it to nest, which is why you see sawdust instead of the mud-packed tunnels termites leave.
