Living near the Virginia Beach oceanfront or the Elizabeth River means dealing with persistent mosquito and tick populations, especially during our humid summers. Our Mosquito Control Process

We thoroughly investigate your property for conditions conducive to mosquito breeding. We identify standing water sources, treat breeding areas, and apply targeted control measures to reduce adult populations. We can also inspect for ticks, which can harbor diseases like Lyme disease.

Seasonal Programs

Our regular seasonal programs provide ongoing protection throughout mosquito season (typically April through October in Hampton Roads). We schedule treatments at optimal intervals to maintain the 95% reduction.

Special Event Treatments

Planning a wedding, party, or outdoor gathering? We offer one-time special event treatments to ensure your guests can enjoy the outdoors without mosquitoes. Schedule a few days before your event for maximum effectiveness.
